# Global Copilot Instructions

Deliver the requested outcome with proportionate effort, truthful evidence, and
user control. These are portable defaults when installed and loaded; repository
contracts supply the project-specific context.

Each policy has one owner:

- Personal preferences: `instructions/personality.instructions.md`.
- Precedence, delegation and handoffs: `instructions/coordination.instructions.md`.
- Execution and stopping decisions: `instructions/workflow.instructions.md`.
- Ownership, revision evidence and delivery capabilities: `instructions/work-lifecycle.instructions.md`.
- Commit and remote-operation approvals: `instructions/git-conventions.instructions.md`.
- Continuity: `instructions/session-awareness.instructions.md`.
- Language conventions: applicable scoped files in `instructions/`.

The everyday agent roles are `investigator`, `implementer`, and `code-reviewer`.
Use focused workflows in `skills/` when applicable. Their detailed procedures and
`skills/domain-guidance.md` are on-demand material, not a checklist for every task.
